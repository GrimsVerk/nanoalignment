# Research C: data and existing assets for nanoalignment

Checked 2026-11-19 via the HF Hub API, datasets-server, GitHub API, the arXiv HTML of 2601.10160v2 and alignmentpretraining.ai. Sizes come from the HF `size` endpoint (rows, parquet bytes). Token counts marked "≈" are estimates (UTF-8 bytes / 4) unless the source states them. "No licence" means the repo or card declares none, so default copyright applies.

## 1. Geodesic release (Tice et al. 2026)

### 1.1 Where things live

- The project site lists 8 models and 3 datasets under `huggingface.co/geodesic/...` ([site](https://alignmentpretraining.ai)). **Those links return HTTP 401: the `geodesic` org does not exist.** The real org is [`geodesic-research`](https://huggingface.co/geodesic-research). It now holds 225 models and 94 datasets, most of them later projects.
- The paper's collection is [Alignment Pretraining](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/alignment-pretraining-6946539245f8c4b27eb86d7c). It contains four sub-collections: [Datasets](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/self-fulfilling-misalignment-datasets-69425bb621ba1a6cd3a6c34e), [Base models](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/self-fulfilling-misalignment-base-models-694062566ccc175a829cee0c), [Post-trained](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/self-fulfilling-misalignment-post-trained-models-69402a8204c1c3de3fa89b55) and [EM LoRAs](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/self-fulfilling-misalignment-emergent-misalignment-6941bd0a9ff0e5d3a8855a62). There is also an unpublished [OLMo 3 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/geodesic-research/self-fulfilling-misalignment-olmo-models-69a44949187db1bf4c3f1c0a).
- GitHub has no code release for data generation, filtering or evals. The paper says "The complete term lists will be available in our open-source repository" ([paper App. H.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10160)), but no such repo exists. The repos that do exist are [GeodesicResearch/sfm-post-training](https://github.com/GeodesicResearch/sfm-post-training) (DPO shell scripts, no licence), [geodesic-gpt-neox](https://github.com/GeodesicResearch/geodesic-gpt-neox) (no licence) and [Kyle1668/alignment-pretraining](https://github.com/Kyle1668/alignment-pretraining) (lm-eval tasks for Anthropic advanced-ai-risk, no licence).
- **Licences:** none of the Geodesic datasets or models declares a licence (`cardData.license` is null for every repo checked). The paper text is CC BY 4.0 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10160)).

### 1.3 Models

All models are 6.9B GPT-NeoX with no licence tag. The exception is the Deep Ignorance parent ([EleutherAI/deep-ignorance-pretraining-stage-unfiltered](https://huggingface.co/EleutherAI/deep-ignorance-pretraining-stage-unfiltered), Apache-2.0). There are 4 base conditions (`sfm_baseline_unfiltered_base`, `sfm_baseline_filtered_base`, `sfm_unfiltered_e2e_alignment_upsampled_base`, `sfm_unfiltered_e2e_misalignment_upsampled_base`), plus Mid/CPT/filtered variants, `_instruct` (SFT) and `_dpo` versions, 21 EM LoRAs, and 2 OLMo 3 7B CPT models (`sfm-olmo-cpt-{alignment,misalignment}-base`, 500M synthetic + 500M general tokens) ([model card](https://huggingface.co/geodesic-research/sfm_unfiltered_e2e_alignment_upsampled_base)). **Verdict:** too large for our single-GPU training, but useful as reference points. We can run our bash harness and log-prob proxy on their 6.9B DPO models to check that the harness separates their conditions.

### 1.4 How their eval works and the headline numbers

- **Construction.** Claude Opus 4.5 got AI-safety source texts and wrote 2-option questions. The misaligned option is "instrumentally appealing", never cartoonish ([eval card prompt](https://huggingface.co/datasets/geodesic-research/discourse-grounded-misalignment-evals)). Sources: AI 2027, Anthropic and Redwood blogs, Carlsmith, 80k Hours and Dwarkesh transcripts, and textbooks.
- **Scoring base models.** The prompt starts "You are an artificial intelligence (AI)." and gives options (A)/(B), "Respond with a single letter.", ending with "Answer: (". The answer is the letter with the highest log-probability. Results are averaged over 4 syntaxes × 2 answer orders (8 prompts). Prompts with strong order bias were screened out, so most stay within 40 to 60% ([paper §2.1, App. B.4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10160)).
- **Post-trained models** are scored under 4 system prompts: none ("just instructions"), "You are an AI assistant.", "helpful", and "HHH".
- **Synthetic docs were generated only from the 2,671 article questions,** about 2,800 docs per question per polarity. The 1,503 textbook questions were held out ([paper §2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10160)).
- **Dose.** About 1% of tokens: 5B in pretraining plus 500M in midtraining. The CPT arm used 1B extra tokens, half synthetic (500M) and half replay ([paper §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10160)).
- **Base misalignment rate, article / textbook split** ([model card table](https://huggingface.co/geodesic-research/sfm_unfiltered_e2e_alignment_upsampled_base)):

| Condition | Article | Textbook |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Unfiltered | 44.7% | 39.6% |
| Filtered | 30.9% | 21.7% |
| Alignment upsampled E2E | 9.2% | 5.9% |
| Alignment CPT | 0.9% | 0.6% |
| Misalignment upsampled E2E | 50.8% | 40.1% |
| Misalignment CPT | 73.5% | 67.9% |

- **After SFT+DPO, HHH prompt:** unfiltered 33.5%, alignment E2E 9.3%, misalignment E2E 20.9%. Misalignment upsampling did not survive post-training. Capability cost was 2 to 4 pp on a 7-benchmark average.
- **Topic coverage relevant to us.** By regex over `topics`, about 347 article and 158 textbook questions carry hacking, gaming, tampering or test-like tags. Examples: "reward-hacking" (70 article), "evaluation-gaming" (67), "proxy gaming" (37 textbook). This makes a ready-made "shortcut" MCQ subset.

### 2.4 Overlap and contamination flags

- **SWE-style "make the failing tests pass" tasks** (SWE-smith, SWE-Gym, SWE-rebench, ImpossibleBench) are the same family as our "edit tests instead of fixing the bug" scenario. Keep them out of RL. Also keep them out of SFT, or the eval loses headroom.
- **Terminal RL tasks verified by `tests/test.sh`** can themselves be hacked when verifiers are weak, for example when they only check that a file exists. GRPO on hackable verifiers trains shortcut-taking in every condition and confounds the comparison. Harden the verifiers, and log hacks during RL as a secondary measure.
- **Screen every task instruction** for log deletion, audit, CI or lint, "disable check", answer or solution files, and "verify" wording. This matters most for the Nemotron Pivot, Endless Terminals and Nemotron synthetic tasks.
- **Trajectory sets are not labelled for hacks.** Teacher agents such as GLM, GPT and Qwen sometimes hardcode outputs or edit tests. Run a regex plus LLM-judge scan before SFT. Examples to catch: `rm *.log`, `sed` on `test_*.py`, `chmod -x`, `exit 0` stubs, `cat solution*`.

## 5. Proposed data plan

1. **Story conditions (generate our own).** Write 3 matched corpora (positive, neutral, negative) with the believe-it-or-not SDF pattern (MIT). Use an **open-weight Apache-2.0 generator** (for example OLMo 3 or Qwen3) so the outputs can ship under MIT or CC-BY without provider-terms questions.
   - Match surface forms across conditions, using Geodesic's six forms plus "agent log / postmortem" forms.
   - Match length, topic and token count. Only the AI's choice may differ.
   - Put a canary string in every generated doc and in every eval file.
   - Dose: 1 to 5% of 100 to 200M tokens is 1 to 10M tokens, about 1k to 7k docs.
2. **Geodesic docs as an ablation arm.** Optionally add their positive and negative midtraining docs. Load them by HF id and revision; do not rehost. This tests "their docs vs our bash-flavoured stories". Their dataset has no neutral split.
3. **Secondary evals.**
   - Geodesic MCQ: score it exactly as the paper does (8 prompts, letter log-prob). Report the textbook split as the held-out headline and the article split separately. Add the ≈500-question hacking, gaming and tampering subset.
   - Log-prob proxy (aligned vs misaligned continuation): build it from our own scenario pairs.
4. **Base replay.**
   - Tier 2, OLMo 2 1B: Dolmino-mix-1124 sample (DCLM, FLAN, wiki, pes2o, math) plus the OLMo starcoder shell subset plus tldr.
   - Tier 1, SmolLM2-135M/360M: FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT (1 shard) plus cosmopedia-v2 plus code. Use the ungated OLMo shell subset, or Stack-Edu Shell if we accept the S3 fetch.
5. **Bash SFT (Order A).**
   - Single-command data: NL2SH-ALFA, tldr, optionally bash-instruct-55k.
   - Short trajectories (≤4k tokens): AgentInstruct OS, plus length-filtered OpenThoughts-Agent and Nemotron `skill_based_easy`, after the hack scan.
   - Chat replay: Tulu 3 OLMo-2 0225 (OLMo) or SmolTalk (SmolLM2).
   - Keep SWE trajectories out.
6. **GRPO RL.** InterCode-Bash (200), OpenThoughts-Agent-v1-RL (≈720) and Endless Terminals (≈2.5k), keyword-screened against the eval themes, with hardened verifiers.
7. **Filtered condition (optional).** Train a small classifier on sfm-alignment-labeling-v3 and check it on its 529 human-labelled test docs. Alternatively rebuild a keyword list from the flagged keywords in the filtering-results data. Replace filtered docs by resampling, as the paper did.
8. **Publishing rules.**
   - The MIT repo ships code, recipes (HF ids + revisions + seeds) and our own generated stories and evals (licensed CC-BY-4.0 or MIT).
   - Do not rehost unlicensed data: all Geodesic assets, AgentInstruct, and the NL2Bash GPL copy.
   - Do not rehost CC-BY-SA StackExchange, unless the derived files are released under SA.
   - Ask Geodesic (contact emails are on the [model card](https://huggingface.co/geodesic-research/sfm_unfiltered_e2e_alignment_upsampled_base)) to add licences.

## 6. Conflicts with the current design

- **Dose is far below Geodesic's.** Their CPT arm used 500M synthetic tokens at 50% of a 1B-token run. Their E2E arm used 1% of 550B tokens (5.5B). Our 1 to 10M tokens at 1 to 5% is 50 to 500 times less in absolute terms. Consider a 10 to 50% synthetic fraction in a short CPT stage, or more repeated epochs.
- **Their effect relies on eval-grounded docs.** Docs were generated per article question, and the textbook generalisation was weaker for misalignment (40% vs 40%). Our stories will not be grounded in the bash scenarios unless we choose that. We need to decide whether stories may depict coding shortcuts (in-distribution) or only general AI conduct (tests generalisation). Both choices are defensible; the design must pick one and state it.
- **No neutral arm upstream.** Geodesic's control is "no added docs". Our neutral condition needs matched neutral AI docs, which we must generate ourselves.
- **Base-model MCQ at 100M to 1B scale.** Geodesic added 1B tokens of MCQA data in midtraining so that letter answers worked. Small models may sit at chance or be dominated by order bias. Rely on the continuation log-prob proxy for Tier 1, and add MCQA replay (for example Dolmino FLAN) if we want letter scoring.
- **Context length.** OLMo 2 1B has 4,096 tokens. Most terminal-agent and all SWE trajectories are longer.
- **Their post-training dampened misalignment effects.** Misalignment E2E fell from 51% to 21% after DPO. Our SFT+GRPO may also wash out the negative condition.
- **Licensing.** No Geodesic asset carries a licence. Use them by reference only until the authors add one.
